Privacy

Last updated 7 September 2026.

The hoobiltit app has no account, no login and no analytics. There is no advertising identifier, no third-party SDK and no crash reporter. The app has one optional feature that publishes anything at all — the shared map — and this page is mostly about that. This website is a separate matter: it counts page views, and This website below says exactly how.

Looking up a road

When you drop a pin or drive, the app queries public county, state and federal map services directly from your device. There is no server in between and nothing of ours to log it. Those agencies receive the coordinates of the query, as any map service must; their own privacy practices are their own.

Reports you keep to yourself

Rating a road stores the report on your device: the exact coordinate, the exact time, your rating, the issue, your note, and the road as it was identified. None of that leaves your device unless you choose to share it, and you can export or delete it at any time.

The shared map

Each report has a Add to the shared map switch. It is off every time — there is no remembered preference, and a report already filed is never published afterwards.

When you turn it on, exactly this is published:

  • your condition rating, one of the county's five values
  • the issue category, one of the county's own maintenance categories
  • the county's published rating for that road, where one exists
  • the road name, its segment identifier, the jurisdiction and the maintaining agency — all of which are public records already
  • a location rounded to about 100 metres
  • the date, with no time of day

And this is never published:

  • your note — the only free text in the app, and it stays on your device
  • the exact coordinate, or the cross streets, which would sharpen it again
  • the time of day
  • your name, email, Apple ID, or any device identifier

The rounding is deliberate and it is the protection. At about 100 metres a suburban lot frontage sits inside one grid square, so a report cannot say which house it was filed outside — while the marker still lands on the right road. That distance is inside the app's own idea of "the road you meant", which is 150 metres.

Shared reports are public. Anyone with the app can see them, including people who are not signed in to iCloud. They are kept indefinitely, because a road's condition over time is the point.

Two honest limits. Apple's servers stamp each record with the time it was received, and we cannot suppress that — the day-only date is what the app shows, and the location rounding is what actually protects you. And rounding is not anonymity: on a rural road, one report in a 100 metre square may still point at one household. That is why the note and the cross streets are not published, and it is why the switch is off by default.

Who processes it

The shared map is Apple's CloudKit, in this app's public database. We run no server of our own and hold no copy. Apple's handling is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Removing a shared report

Open Your reports, swipe the report and choose Remove from the shared map. It is deleted from the pool. One limitation worth stating plainly: this works from the device that filed it, because that is where the record of having shared it lives. We deliberately do not keep an index of who published what — doing so would mean building the very identity link this design avoids.

Reporting a marker

Tapping any shared marker offers Report this marker. A marker reported by several people stops being shown. There is no free text in a shared report, so there is nothing obscene one can contain; flagging is for markers that are wrong or misplaced. Anything else, write to us.

This website

hoobiltit.com uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-L46G8VYPYH) to count visits and see which pages people read. Google sets cookies in your browser for this and processes your IP address and basic device information on our behalf. It applies to pages on hoobiltit.com only — the app contains no analytics SDK, is not connected to this, and sends nothing to Google.

The site uses Google Consent Mode. In practice:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics storage is off until you agree. Nothing is written to your browser and no identified measurement happens unless you press Accept on the notice.
  • Everywhere else, analytics is on by default and you can switch it off at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer of any page. Declining also deletes any _ga cookies already set.
  • Advertising signals are denied everywhere, always. No ad storage, no ad personalisation, no ad user data. This site shows no ads.

Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage, not on a server.

Children

hoobiltit is not directed at children and knowingly collects nothing from them.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything that alters what is published will be described in the app before it takes effect.
