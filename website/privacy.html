Privacy

Last updated 8 September 2026.

The hoobiltit app has no account, no login and no analytics. There is no advertising identifier, no third-party SDK and no crash reporter. It has no server of ours to talk to, records nothing about you, and publishes nothing anywhere. This page says what that means in practice, and covers the analytics on this website, which is a separate thing.

Looking up a road

When you drop a pin or drive, the app queries public county, state and federal map services directly from your device. There is no server in between and nothing of ours to log it. Those agencies receive the coordinates of the query, as any map service must; their own privacy practices are their own.

What the app records about you

Nothing. hoobiltit keeps no reports, no history and no profile of you. Earlier versions let you rate roads and, briefly, share those ratings on a map; both were removed, and the first launch after that change deletes anything a previous version had stored on your device.

The app does keep a short-lived cache of road lookups so it does not re-ask the same question repeatedly. It lives on your device, it expires, and it holds public map data — not you.

Sharing

You can share a road as a card, as a full report, as a drafted records request, or export the roads from a drive. Sharing happens only when you tap share, and it goes wherever you send it — we are not involved and receive no copy.

Cards, reports and the drive-log export deliberately carry no coordinates. The one exception is the public-records request, which cites the exact location because the agency you are writing to needs it to identify the road; that letter goes only where you send it.

Who processes it

For the app: nobody. Lookups go straight from your device to the public county, state and federal map services that answer them, with nothing of ours in between. Those agencies receive the coordinates of the query, as any map service must, and their own practices are their own.

This website

hoobiltit.com uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-L46G8VYPYH) to count visits and see which pages people read. Google sets cookies in your browser for this and processes your IP address and basic device information on our behalf. It applies to pages on hoobiltit.com only — the app contains no analytics SDK, is not connected to this, and sends nothing to Google.

The site uses Google Consent Mode. In practice:

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics storage is off until you agree. Nothing is written to your browser and no identified measurement happens unless you press Accept on the notice.
  • Everywhere else, analytics is on by default and you can switch it off at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer of any page. Declining also deletes any _ga cookies already set.
  • Advertising signals are denied everywhere, always. No ad storage, no ad personalisation, no ad user data. This site shows no ads.

Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage, not on a server.

Children

hoobiltit is not directed at children and knowingly collects nothing from them.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything that alters what is published will be described in the app before it takes effect.
